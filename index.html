A MESSAGE FROM OUR HEADTEACHER

Headteacher's Message

Dear students, respected guardians and well-wishers, I extend my heartfelt greetings to you all.

Education is the foundation of a thoughtful, capable and humane society. At Mokhleshpur High School, our goal is to create a supportive learning environment where every student can develop knowledge, sound character, discipline and confidence.

We encourage our students to be curious, diligent and respectful, and to use what they learn for the good of their families, community and country. We believe that academic achievement is most meaningful when it goes hand in hand with honesty, compassion and responsibility.

I invite teachers, guardians, former students and well-wishers to work together for the continued progress of our school. With your support and the dedication of our teachers, may every student move towards a brighter future.

Let us move forward together in the light of education.

Mohammad Emran Ali Headteacher Mokhleshpur High School
